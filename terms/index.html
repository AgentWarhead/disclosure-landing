What Disclosure is

DISCLOSURE is a civilian first contact preparation app, in development for iOS and Android, with this website as its public file. The app is not available yet, and no release date is set. Today the site offers a ten-question classification, a dossier for each role, a first contact briefing, a readiness drill and an archive of intel files.

DISCLOSURE provides entertainment, education, scenario training and preparation content. Where the site describes reported sightings, beings or lore, it is describing what people have reported, not established fact.

DISCLOSURE is not affiliated with any government, military, intelligence agency, emergency service, scientific authority or official first contact body. It is not official emergency guidance, and it is not medical, legal or survival advice or a substitute for professional judgment. In a real emergency, follow your local authorities.

Your role and your card

Your role, serial number and iris print are generated from your answers for entertainment and preparation inside DISCLOSURE. The First Contact Card is not an identity document, license, certification or security clearance, and it must not be presented as one. Your serial number is random and does not identify you.
