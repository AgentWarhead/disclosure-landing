# DISCLOSURE: SEO gaps, competitor map and tool ideas

Researched 2026-09-28. Web search, web fetch and live Google autocomplete only. No paid keyword tool was available (the Ahrefs and Similarweb connectors are not authorized in this session), so **no search volumes are given anywhere: every volume is "volume unknown"**. Demand evidence comes from three sources:

- **AC**: live Google autocomplete, fetched 2026-09-28 from `suggestqueries.google.com`. Autocomplete only shows queries with real, repeated demand, but it gives no volume. The suggestions came back geo-weighted to Canada (they mention Ontario, Toronto, Nova Scotia and Calgary), so they reflect a Canadian searcher. That fits Brett's market.
- **SERP**: what currently ranks, taken from web search results.
- **News**: dated coverage that shows why interest spiked.

## 0. The three findings that matter most

1. **The brand name collides with a Spielberg film.** *Disclosure Day* (Universal, directed by Spielberg from a David Koepp script) opened in the US on 2026-06-12 and grossed about $241M worldwide ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disclosure_Day); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/timlammers/2026/07/18/spielbergs-disclosure-day-streaming-date-nears-after-underwhelming-232-million-box-office-take/)). Autocomplete for "is disclosure day" returns **"is disclosure day real"** and **"is disclosure day based on a book"** (AC). "Age of disclosure documentary where to watch" and "age of disclosure spielberg" also appear (AC). Anyone searching the film is one step away from the brand. An honest "Is Disclosure Day real? What the public record actually says" page is the cheapest traffic on this list. Do not use studio imagery or suggest any affiliation.
2. **The tracker lane filled up in 2026.** At least eight new sites launched around PURSUE and now compete for "UFO files" and "disclosure timeline" queries: [UFO Files Watch](https://ufofileswatch.com/), [UAP Radar](https://uapradar.com/timeline), [UAP Disclosure Tracker](https://uapdisclosuretracker.com/), [UFO Data Live](https://www.ufodatalive.com/), [UFO-Track countdown](https://ufo-track.com/countdown/), [uap.global](https://uap.global/2026-uap-file-releases-timeline/), [Now Declassified](https://www.nowdeclassified.com/archive) and [SimulationMaps](https://www.simulationmaps.com/ufo-sightings-map). The Black Vault also shipped a [searchable PURSUE archive](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/the-black-vault-launches-searchable-ufo-files-release-archive/). A plain tracker will not win. DISCLOSURE can win on **verdict honesty**: the VERIFIED / PARTLY / FALSE labels already in `docs/research-timeline.md` are something none of them show. It can also win on **the civilian's next step** (drill, identifier, report builder), which the trackers do not offer.
3. **"What did I just see" is the largest evergreen tool intent, and tools already rank for it.** Autocomplete for "what is that light in the sky" runs to tonight, bright, looks like a star, flashing, green, blue, orange and purple (AC). Orbital Radar ranks for it with a 2 to 4 tap decision tree plus a live checker ([orbitalradar.com](https://orbitalradar.com/what-is-that-light-in-the-sky)). The Enigma app has an AR "Identify Lens" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/enigma-whats-that-in-the-sky/id1548371173)). A better tool alone will not win this. DISCLOSURE's angle is the chain: identify, then drill, then log, then report.

## 3. Tool and experience ideas

Complexity: **S** is about 1 to 3 days on the static site, **M** about 1 to 2 weeks, **L** longer or needs a backend.

### 3.1 "What did I just see?" sky identifier (M, flagship)
- **What:** The user enters time, location (or taps "now, here"), compass direction, elevation, and behaviour (steady / blinking / train / fading / fast streak). The tool returns ranked candidates with a plain reason for each: ISS pass, Starlink train, other satellite, Venus, Jupiter, bright star, aircraft, meteor/fireball, recent rocket launch. The last screen ends with "Still unexplained? Log it" (links to 3.4) and "Run the drill".
- **Demand:** Cluster A (AC-confirmed stems).
- **Honest data:** Satellite positions come from CelesTrak GP elements run through satellite.js, client side. CelesTrak's policy since March 2026 is one download per update cycle (about 2 h) and a firewall block above 100 MB/day ([policy](https://celestrak.org/usage-policy.php)), so a scheduled job should cache the Starlink, ISS and brightest groups as a static JSON file. Planets and stars come from a local ephemeris library (astronomy-engine, MIT). Aircraft are optional, from adsb.lol, which needs no key and is licensed ODbL 1.0 ([API docs](https://www.adsb.lol/docs/open-data/api/)); ODbL needs attribution and share-alike on derived databases. Meteor shower calendars come from the IMO/AMS. ISS cross-check links go to NASA Spot the Station.
- **Avoid:** OpenSky, which needs a written licence for commercial or operational use ([terms](https://opensky-network.org/about/terms-of-use)). Heavens-Above has no public API: link to it, do not scrape it. N2YO allows 1,000 transactions/hour on a key ([wrapper docs](https://github.com/djtimca/hasatellitetracker)); fine as a fallback, but client-side propagation is cheaper and needs no key.
- **Flags:** Never output "UFO" as a result. The honest last bucket is "no match in public data". Location stays on the device.

### 3.2 Live public-record tracker, "The Disclosure Ledger" (S to M)
- **What:** Every official event (hearings, laws, AARO reports, NARA transfers, each PURSUE release), each with a date, primary source link and a **verdict label** (VERIFIED / PARTLY / NOT FOUND / FALSE AS WORDED). Each PURSUE release gets its own child page with counts and notable files.
- **Demand:** Clusters B and C.
- **Data:** war.gov/UFO (US government works; check each file's page for third-party material), NARA RG 615 and its Catalog API with JSON bulk metadata ([API](https://www.archives.gov/research/catalog/help/api)), aaro.mil, congress.gov, govinfo.gov. Seed data already exists in `docs/research-timeline.md`.
- **Flags:** At least 8 competitors exist; the verdict column is the moat. Keep "the Act passed" wording exact.

### 3.3 Case Files database with map and timeline (M content, S to M UI)
- **What:** Structured case pages (date, place, witnesses, official finding, leading explanations, what is still open), browsable on a map and a timeline. Start with Cluster D's 18 cases plus the 4 existing ones.
- **Demand:** Cluster D (AC confirms Gimbal, Jellyfish, NJ drones, Ariel, Walton, Shag Harbour).
- **Data:** Primary records (Blue Book at NARA, AARO case resolutions, PURSUE files, the Navy's 2020 video release), with Wikipedia used only to find the primary sources.
- **Flags:** Name living witnesses only when they are already public. State claims as claims.

### 3.4 Witness report builder, output as a clean PDF (S to M)
- **What:** A guided form (time, place, direction, duration, angular size by the "fist at arm's length" method, weather, other witnesses, media hashes) that generates a PDF and a plain-text copy. It tells the user where to send it: NUFORC and MUFON in the US, the Canadian UFO Survey and the Sky Canada public reporting page in Canada, the AMS for fireballs. It is honest that AARO takes no civilian reports.
- **Demand:** Cluster G ("ufo sighting report website", "report ufo sighting canada", AC).
- **Data:** None stored. Build it fully client side (jsPDF).
- **Flags:** Privacy: no upload, no location sent anywhere, a warning before sharing EXIF, a note for minors. Do not pre-fill or auto-submit third-party forms; link to them.

### 3.5 "Is Disclosure Day real?" plus a fiction-versus-record explainer (S)
- **What:** A single strong page setting the film's premise against the public record, with links into the Ledger and the quiz.
- **Demand:** AC ("is disclosure day real", "based on a book").
- **Flags:** Trademark: name the film factually, no posters or stills, no "official" wording. Time sensitive while the film streams (it moved to Peacock per [Movie Insider](https://www.movieinsider.com/m24937/disclosure-day)).

### 3.6 Drake equation calculator plus Kardashev explorer (S)
- **What:** Sliders with cited ranges for each term (for example, the fraction of stars with planets from exoplanet surveys), a "pessimist / optimist / your guess" presets row, and a share card with your number. Pair it with a Kardashev "where is Earth" gauge.
- **Demand:** The tool SERP is crowded ([Omni](https://www.omnicalculator.com/physics/drake-equation), [PBS NOVA](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/origins/drake.html)); AC confirms Kardashev interest.
- **Data:** NASA Exoplanet Archive for the planet-fraction inputs.
- **Flags:** None. It wins links from teachers; do not expect it to outrank Omni.

### 3.7 Sighting credibility scorer (S; modelled on the Rio Scale)
- **What:** Scores the *report's quality*, not whether it was aliens: number of witnesses, independent sensors, duration, known-object checks run, data preserved. Output is "report strength", with advice on how to raise it.
- **Demand:** Volume unknown. It fits `rio-scale-explained` and `ufo-evidence-checklist`.
- **Flags:** Wording is the whole risk. Never output "likely alien". Credit the SETI/IAA Rio Scale as inspiration, not endorsement.

### 3.8 "Day X since disclosure began" counter tied to real legislation (S)
- **What:** A counter from a defined, sourced start (for example, 2023-07-26 sworn testimony, or 2026-05-08 PURSUE Release 1), plus a "next scheduled event" card (Senate NDAA conference, next AARO deadline, next PURSUE tranche if announced).
- **Demand:** Countdown pages exist ([UFO-Track](https://ufo-track.com/countdown/)). Volume unknown.
- **Flags:** Never count *down* to an invented disclosure date. Label anything that has not been announced as "no date announced".

### 3.9 Species lore atlas (M)
- **What:** One browsable atlas over the existing species pages (Greys, Nordics, Mantids, Reptilians, Pleiadians, Arcturians, Sirians, Tall Whites, Anunnaki, Insectoids), showing origin of the lore, first appearance in print, pop-culture spread, and a "status: lore, unverified" badge.
- **Demand:** Consolidates existing pages; volume unknown.
- **Flags:** Keep the "reports and lore" framing on every card.

### 3.10 Glossary (S)
- **What:** 80 to 120 terms (UAP, NHI, AARO, PURSUE, RG 615, transmedium, five observables, CE1 to CE5, Rio Scale, technosignature), each with a one-line definition and a link to the deeper page. DefinedTerm schema.
- **Demand:** UAPedia has one ([glossary](https://uapedia.ai/wiki/glossary/)); it feeds AI answers and the internal link mesh.

### 3.11 Sightings map (L; only with a licence)

- **Flags:** The Black Vault, UFO Files Watch and UAP Disclosure Tracker already do this. Summarise and link rather than mirror. Low marginal value.
