## Safe to publish (rock solid, primary sources, by date)

1. **July 26, 2023**: Former military officers testified under oath before a House Oversight subcommittee about UAP. (oversight.house.gov / congress.gov)
2. **September 14, 2023**: NASA's independent UAP study team published its final report. (nasa.gov)
3. **December 22, 2023**: The FY2024 defense bill became law and ordered a UAP Records Collection at the National Archives. (congress.gov, archives.gov)
4. **March 8, 2024**: The Pentagon's AARO published Volume 1 of its historical review of the government's UAP record. (defense.gov / aaro.mil)
5. **November 13, 2024**: A second House Oversight hearing, "Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Exposing the Truth". (oversight.house.gov / congress.gov)
6. **April 24, 2025**: The National Archives released UAP records transferred from four federal agencies. (archives.gov)

Next tier, safe with the correct wording: **Feb 19, 2026**, the President directed agencies to release UAP files (a Truth Social post, not an executive order). **May 8, 2026**, the Department of War published its first PURSUE batch of UAP files at war.gov/UFO (not the National Archives).

## Fixes the page needs

- Replace "Trump executive order (Feb 2026)" with "Feb 2026: the President directed agencies to identify and release UAP files". Calling it an executive order is false.
- Replace "May 8, 2026: National Archives UAP bulk downloads went live" with "May 8, 2026: the Department of War published its first batch of declassified UAP files". The National Archives bulk downloads existed in 2024.
- Do not say "the UAP Disclosure Act passed". A cut-down version became law in Dec 2023, and the full act passed the House only in July 2026.
- Every official report cited here (AARO, NASA) says no evidence of extraterrestrial technology was found. The page can say the topic is no longer fringe. It must not suggest these records confirm contact.
