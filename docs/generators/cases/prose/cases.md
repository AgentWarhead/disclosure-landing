How each case is labeled

Every case carries one of three labels. The label describes the state of the evidence today. It can change when the record does.

Explained

An official finding or a well-documented cause accounts for the main evidence. Loose ends may remain.

Disputed

An ordinary explanation is on the table with real support, and credible people still reject it, or the record is too thin to settle it.

Unexplained

No explanation fits the record yet. The label says nobody has shown what it was, and nothing more.
