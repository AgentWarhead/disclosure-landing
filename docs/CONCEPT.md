## 2. The ground

The customer lives on a phone at night: a black room, one light source (the screen), the feeling that something is watching back. Ground = the void (near-black with a cold green cast). Artifact material, reserved for artifact objects only = cold grey photocopy paper with toner-black ink and black redaction bars. Paper never becomes a page background; it sits ON the void like a leaked page on a dark desk.

## 3. The concept sentence

> The site is a leaked civilian contact file, calm government paperwork about an impossible thing, and while you read it, something on the other side is reading you back.

The eeriness is calm, not loud. Bureaucratic composure about the impossible (the SCP, Control, X-Files register), never neon HUD noise. Green is a signal that something is live, used under 10% of any viewport.

## 5. Register

Tone dark x mood technical-eerie. Estate neighbours: Smokefall (cinematic-dark, warm). Disclosure differs by ground (cold void + photocopy paper), by type voice (US federal Public Sans + Space Mono), and by colour (one CRT signal green inherited from the app).

## 6. The three signatures

1. Visual world: the void and the file. Photocopied pages on black, redactions that lift, one line that never does.
2. Interaction (showpiece): The Iris Print. See section 7.
3. Typographic voice: Public Sans, the typeface of US federal websites (concept reason: this is the government's own paperwork, leaked), set heavy and calm for poster lines and plain for reading. Space Mono (the app's mono) as the typewriter and document-control voice. Two families.

Connective-tissue kit (named, repeated on every page):
1. Redaction: real black bars in copy. Some lift once when read. One per page never lifts.
2. The Watcher: the small eye mark in the header. The pupil follows the pointer, it blinks rarely, it closes when you leave the tab.
3. Control line: each page ends with a document control strip (file number, release status) in Space Mono.
4. Released-in-part stamp: the only red, only on paper objects.

## 7. The showpiece: The Iris Print

1. Anxiety: "This is just another BuzzFeed quiz with a generic result."
2. Gesture from the world: a biometric intake. They scan your iris.
3. Performance on real data: every answer grows a real iris beside the question. Fibre colour is the running weight of your four role scores, rings and crypts come from which answer you picked, the seed is your exact answer sequence. Two people with different answers never get the same iris. The final iris goes on the First Contact Card as a downloadable PNG.
4. Caption: "It was reading you the whole time."
5. Name: The Iris Print.

Class: input-seeded generative artifact (identity rendered from your own choices). Not in the showpiece register. Lives on the home classification; /quiz/ mounts the same engine for search visitors who land there (stated reason: a searcher for "alien encounter quiz" must be able to take it where they land). Foreshadowed within 3 seconds by the hero eye itself. Reduced motion: the iris renders its final state per answer with no growth tween. Keyboard: every option is a real button. Live region announces each answer and the result.

## 8. Page score (home)

An eye that opens and settles on you as you scroll, a photocopied record whose redactions lift as you read it, an iris that grows from your answers, and a fifth designation that stays blacked out.
