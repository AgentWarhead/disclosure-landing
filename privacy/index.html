Privacy policy.

What this site keeps about you, where it goes, and how to have it removed.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

File DSC-903 / Privacy / UnclassifiedReleased in partCivilian copy
The short version
  • Your classification answers stay in your own browser unless you choose to send your card by email.
  • If you do, we keep your email, role and serial, send you one card email, and later send launch news. Nothing else. You can unsubscribe from any email.
  • Google Analytics counts visits with IP anonymization and no advertising features.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not look up where you are.

The classification

The ten questions run entirely in your browser. Your answers, the role they produce, a random serial number and the date it was issued are saved in your browser’s local storage, under the key dx-file-v2, together with the small bits of state the page needs to show your result again, such as the order the questions appeared in. That is why your result is still there when you come back.

The iris print is drawn from your answers on your own device. Nothing from the classification is sent to us unless you submit your email. To erase it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

If you send your email

When you enter your email to receive your First Contact Card, we store three things in our database: your email address, your role and your serial number, along with the time they were added. The database runs on Supabase, in our waitlist table.

We then send your card by email through Resend, from team@getdisclosure.app. Later, we may send news about the app’s launch. The form says exactly what you are agreeing to:

One card email now, launch news later, nothing else. Unsubscribe from any email.

That is the whole list. We do not add your address to anything else, and we do not share it or sell it.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization turned on. It loads only after the page has finished loading and the browser is idle. It records page views and a few events: when a classification starts, when it is completed (with the role it produced), when someone signs up, saves their card, shares it, or turns the ambient sound on or off.

No event carries your email address or your answers. Google’s advertising features are not turned on. Google Analytics sets its own cookies to tell one visit from another. You can block it with a content blocker or with Google’s opt-out browser add-on, and the site works the same without it.

Fonts, sound and cookies

The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google’s servers, as it does on many sites.

The optional ambient sound is generated in your browser. Nothing is recorded. Your sound preference is kept in your browser’s session storage and is cleared when you close the tab.

The site sets no cookies of its own. The only cookies are the ones Google Analytics sets.

What we do not collect

  • We do not look up your location. An earlier version of this site guessed your city from your IP address. That lookup has been removed.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number or anything beyond an email address, and the email is optional.
  • We do not use advertising pixels, and we do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • This site does not use your camera or microphone.

These services process data to run the site. They are not given permission to sell it.

How long we keep it

Classification data stays in your browser until you clear it. Your email, role and serial stay on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Google Analytics keeps event data for the retention period set in our account, which Google caps at 14 months.

Unsubscribe

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and it brings you to this section. To be removed, email team@getdisclosure.app with the subject “unsubscribe” from the address you want removed, or reply to any of our emails with that word. Your address is removed from the list.

If you would rather we delete your record entirely, say so in the same email.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send requests, including deletion requests, to team@getdisclosure.app. We handle reasonable requests as applicable privacy law requires.

Children

DISCLOSURE is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us their email address, contact us and we will review it and delete it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards to protect the data we hold. No system is perfectly secure. Keeping as little as possible is part of the protection: there is less to lose.

The app

The DISCLOSURE app is in development for iOS and Android and is not available yet. This policy covers this website. Before the app opens, this page will be updated to cover what the app handles.

Changes and contact

If what we collect changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Questions about your data go to team@getdisclosure.app. The rules for using the site are in the terms of service.

The paperwork ends here.

The classification runs in your browser, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to send your card. How the site handles accessibility is on the record too.
