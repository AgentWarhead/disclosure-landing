Is it a satellite, a drone, or something else?

Most lights in the sky have ordinary explanations. These files teach you to rule them out fast, so the rare one that remains gets taken seriously.

7 files · Satellite, drone or something else

Archive / Field guide / 7 filesReleased in partCivilian copy

The short answer

Most UFO sightings turn out to be satellites, aircraft, drones, balloons, planets or camera artifacts. To identify one, note its speed, direction, blinking pattern, sound and how long it stayed visible, then check each ordinary explanation in turn. What survives that check is worth reporting.

Misidentification is not an insult to witnesses. It is the first filter every serious investigator uses, and running it yourself is what makes your report credible when something does not fit.
