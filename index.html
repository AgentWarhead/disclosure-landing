The file finds your role. The app trains it.

DISCLOSURE is a phone app built for the minute nobody rehearses. It is in development for iOS and Android. Your card holds your place.

Signal language
  1. Liaison Standard

    Hands that say no threat.

    The front camera watches your hands while you practice a small set of open, slow gestures until they hold without thinking.

  2. Universal Translator

    A first hello in math.

    Frequency tones from the speaker and pulses from the flashlight, in patterns built to read as deliberate and not as a threat.

  3. Stone Cold Drills

    Train the freeze out.

    Strange audio and sudden light while the phone measures how still you stay. The drill ends when your hands stop shaking before your mind does.

  4. First Contact Card

    Carry your designation.

    Your role, readiness score and serial on a digital card that lives on your phone. The one you get today is the first issue.
