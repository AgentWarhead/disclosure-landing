The file finds your role. The app trains it.

DISCLOSURE is a phone app built for the minute nobody rehearses. It is in development for iOS and Android. It launches with five things. The classification, your card and your first signs are free. Clearance unlocks unlimited drills, crew drills and a card that grows. Your card holds your place.

The Classification
  1. The Classification

    Ten answers. An eye nobody else has.

    Ten questions about the first minute of contact grow an iris from your answers and name the job you would reach for first. You can take it on this page now; the app plays your eye back as a six-second video.

  2. Don’t Flinch

    Hold still. Keep the clip.

    Sudden sound and light while the front camera films your face and the phone scores how still you stay. You keep the clip and the score, and your crew gets a number to beat. An optional minute of breathing, paced by haptics, comes first. So does a warning, and there is no fast strobe.

  3. The Open Hand

    Six signs. Master them all.

    From the Open Hand to your role’s own sign, the phone tracks your hands on the device itself and tells you when each one is clean. Then play Echo: mirror the sequence before time runs out, and film yourself doing it.

  4. Crew

    Four roles. Who is missing?

    Invite your household or your friends. Each person’s role comes from their own classification, the crew card shows the mix and names the gap, and crew drills give every phone its owner’s job.

  5. First Contact Card

    A card that grows with you.

    Your role, serial and iris on a card that lives on your phone. Every drill adds a mark to it. The one you get today is the first issue.
