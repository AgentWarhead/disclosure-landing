What the ten questions measure.

Every question is a scene from the same night, and every scene gives you four moves. None of them is wrong. Each one leans toward a different first job: protect the people, calm the room, keep the record, or find the way out.

Your designation is the job you reached for most often. When two jobs tie, the later and harder scenes decide it. The options are shuffled for every visitor, so the first answer on the screen is never a hint.

The iris beside the questions is built from the same answers. Its color follows your running mix of the four jobs, and its rings and markings come from which moves you picked. Two people who answer differently never finish with the same one.

Questions about the quiz.

Plain answers for anyone deciding whether to spend a few minutes on it.

What is the alien encounter quiz?

A ten-question first contact quiz. Each question is a scene from one night of a contact event, and each answer leans toward one of four civilian roles. You finish with a designation, a serial and an iris print built from your answers.

Is it a first contact personality test?

Close. It never asks you to describe yourself. It asks what you would do under uncertainty, stress and a crowd, and names the job you reach for first. It is a preparedness exercise, not a psychological assessment.

What results can I get?

Sentinel, Diplomat, Scholar or Survivor. Each has a dossier that explains the job and how to train it. A fifth designation, First Contact, is not issued. It appears.

How many questions are there?

Ten. Each one is a pressure scene with four possible moves, and most people finish in a few minutes.

Is it useful for a real UFO or UAP sighting?

The habits it rewards, like staying calm, keeping your distance and taking clean notes, apply to any strange sighting. For a real one, read the intel file on what to do if you see a UFO.

What happens after I get my result?

You can save your First Contact Card as an image, share it, or enter your email to have the card sent to you. The same email gets launch access when the app opens.

Are my answers saved?

Your answers are kept in this browser so your result is still here when you come back. Your email is only collected if you type it in and ask for your card.
